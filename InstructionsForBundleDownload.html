Printable Bundle Instructions

How to Download Your Bundle

Thank you for your purchase! Your printable bundle includes multiple digital products designed for easy downloading and printing.

  1. Complete your purchase through Payhip.
  2. Download the ZIP file or PDF files included with your purchase.
  3. If your download is a ZIP file, extract (unzip) it to access all included files.
  4. Open each PDF using your preferred PDF reader.
  5. Print each item on US Letter (8.5" × 11") paper or cardstock, depending on the product.
  6. Select Actual Size or Fit to Page, depending on your printer.
  7. Follow the trimming or assembly instructions for each printable, if needed.
  8. Enjoy your printable bundle!

Printing Tips
