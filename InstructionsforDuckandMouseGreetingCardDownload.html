Printable Greeting Card Instructions

How to Download Your Greeting Card

Thank you for your purchase! Follow these simple steps to download, print, and assemble your Duck & Mouse Greeting Card.

  1. Complete your purchase through Payhip.
  2. Download the ZIP file to your computer, tablet, or phone.
  3. Extract the ZIP file to access your PDF files.
  4. Open the PDF using your preferred PDF reader.
  5. Print on US Letter (8.5" × 11") cardstock for the best results.
  6. Select Actual Size or 100% Scale when printing.
  7. Carefully cut along the trim lines if needed.
  8. Fold the card along the center fold to create the finished greeting card.
  9. Write your personal message inside and enjoy!

Printing Tips

  • Heavyweight cardstock produces the best quality greeting card.
  • Use a paper cutter for clean, straight edges.
  • Score the fold line before folding for a crisp, professional finish.
  • Print as many copies as you'd like for your personal use.
