Printable Wall Art Instructions

How to Download Your Wall Art

Thank you for your purchase! Follow these simple steps to download and print your Woodland Duck & Mouse Wall Art.

  1. Complete your purchase through Payhip.
  2. Download the ZIP file to your computer, tablet, or phone.
  3. Extract the ZIP file to access your PDF, PNG, and JPG files.
  4. Choose the file format that works best for your printer or print service.
  5. Print at 100% scale or Actual Size for the best results.
  6. Use high-quality paper, cardstock, or photo paper for a professional finish.
  7. Frame your artwork or display it however you like.
  8. Enjoy your new Woodland Duck & Mouse Wall Art!

Printing Tips

  • Photo paper provides vibrant colors and sharp details.
  • Heavyweight matte cardstock creates a beautiful art print finish.
  • You can print at home or through a local or online print shop.
  • Print as many copies as you'd like for your personal use.
