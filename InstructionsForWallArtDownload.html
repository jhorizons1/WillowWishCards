Printable Wall Art Instructions

How to Download Your Wall Art

Thank you for your purchase! Follow these simple steps to download and print your new wall art.

  1. Complete your purchase through Payhip.
  2. Download the PDF or image file to your computer, tablet, or phone.
  3. Open the file using your preferred PDF viewer or image viewer.
  4. Print using the recommended paper size listed with the product.
  5. Select Actual Size or Fit to Page, depending on your printer and paper size.
  6. Frame or display your finished artwork and enjoy!

Printing Tips

  • Use high-quality matte or satin photo paper for the best appearance.
  • Heavyweight cardstock also produces excellent results.
  • Print using your printer's highest quality setting whenever possible.
  • You may also have your artwork professionally printed at a local print shop.

Personal Use Only

This printable wall art is for personal use only. The files may not be resold, redistributed, shared, or used for commercial purposes.
