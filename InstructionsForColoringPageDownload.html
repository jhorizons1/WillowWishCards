Coloring Page Instructions

How to Download Your Coloring Page

Thank you for your purchase! Follow these simple steps to download and enjoy your printable coloring page.

  1. Complete your purchase through Payhip.
  2. Download the PDF file to your computer, tablet, or phone.
  3. Open the file using your preferred PDF reader.
  4. Print on US Letter (8.5" × 11") paper or cardstock.
  5. Select Actual Size or Fit to Page, depending on your printer.
  6. Color and enjoy!

Printing Tips

  • Standard printer paper works great for crayons and colored pencils.
  • For markers, consider using heavyweight paper or cardstock to reduce bleed-through.
  • Print as many copies as you'd like for your personal use.
