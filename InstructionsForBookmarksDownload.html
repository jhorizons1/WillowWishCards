Printable Bookmark Instructions

How to Download Your Bookmarks

Thank you for your purchase! Follow these simple steps to download and create your printable bookmarks.

  1. Complete your purchase through Payhip.
  2. Download the PDF file to your computer, tablet, or phone.
  3. Open the file using your preferred PDF reader.
  4. Print on US Letter (8.5" × 11") cardstock or heavyweight paper for the best results.
  5. Select Actual Size or Fit to Page, depending on your printer.
  6. Carefully cut along the trim lines using scissors or a paper cutter.
  7. Optional: Laminate your bookmarks for extra durability.
  8. Enjoy your new bookmarks!

Printing Tips

  • Cardstock provides the best durability for bookmarks.
  • Use a paper cutter for clean, straight edges.
  • Laminating your bookmarks can help protect them from wear and tear.
  • Print as many copies as you'd like for your personal use.

Personal Use Only

These printable bookmarks are for personal use only. They may not be resold, redistributed, shared, or used for commercial purposes.
